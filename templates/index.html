Platform Features

                
Key capabilities of the MailGuard AI threat classification platform.

              

            

            

              

                

                  🎯
                  
Calibrated Probabilities

                  
Unlike raw SVM hyperplane distances, Platt scaling produces genuine, calibrated probability estimates.

                

                

                  ⚖️
                  
Uncertainty Band Handling

                  
Predictions falling in the empirical [0.40, 0.60] boundary automatically flag as Needs Review for human verification.

                

                

                  🔎
                  
In-Text Token Highlighting

                  
Spam-driving (▲) and legitimate-driving (▼) n-grams are highlighted directly in the email content with non-color cues.

                

                

                  🛡️
                  
Security & Privacy First

                  
Full CSP, zero innerHTML, self-hosted Poppins fonts, and complete client-side .eml and .txt parsing.

Known Limitations & Constraints

                  
    
                    
  • Historical bias: Trained on Enron corporate communications from the early 2000s; modern scams (e.g. crypto phishing) may have lower coverage.
    • 
                    
  • English-only: Preprocessor strips non-ASCII accents; non-English email yields reduced accuracy.
    • 
                    
  • Short text sensitivity: Very short messages (<10 words) lack sufficient statistical n-grams.
    •
